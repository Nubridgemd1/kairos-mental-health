About Kairos

Care that meets you at the right time.

In Greek, kairos means the opportune moment — the turning point where change becomes possible. That idea is at the heart of our practice. We believe healing happens when skilled, compassionate care meets a person exactly where, and when, they need it.

We provide thorough psychiatric evaluations, thoughtful medication management, and supportive therapy for children, adolescents and adults. Every visit is unhurried and collaborative, built around your goals, your story, and your pace.

Our mission

To make high‑quality, compassionate mental health care accessible and unhurried — meeting each person at their moment, and walking with them toward lasting well‑being.

Ewoma Obaro, PMHNP

Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner

Ewoma Obaro is a Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner dedicated to compassionate, whole‑person care. She partners with each client to understand their story, clarify a diagnosis, and build a thoughtful plan — combining careful psychiatric evaluation and medication management with supportive, goal‑focused therapy. Her approach is warm, unhurried and collaborative, meeting people of all ages right where they are.
